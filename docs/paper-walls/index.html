175 years of evidence

The finding keeps being made, and keeps not being acted on.

  1. 1845Engels: Liverpool average age at death 35 for gentry, 15 for labourers — “social murder”.
  2. 1848Virchow: “Medicine is a social science.”
  3. 1899Du Bois, The Philadelphia Negro: house-to-house survey of 10,000+ residents.
  4. 1906Du Bois: mortality gaps are “not a racial disease but a social disease”.
  5. 1930sHOLC redlining maps grade neighborhoods A to D.
  6. 1974Lalonde Report: the health field concept.
  7. 1978Alma-Ata: inequality “politically, socially and economically unacceptable”.
  8. 1980Black Report: class V infants die at 2× class I; 74,000 avoidable deaths.
  9. 1984Whitehall I: lowest civil-service grade ~3× mortality — the social gradient.
  10. 1986Ottawa Charter: prerequisites — peace, shelter, education, food, income.
  11. 1991Dahlgren–Whitehead rainbow model.
  12. 1995Link & Phelan: fundamental cause theory — money, knowledge, power, connections.
  13. 2005Patient Navigator Act (Pub. L. 109-18).
  14. 2008WHO Commission: “Social injustice is killing people on a grand scale.”
  15. 2010Marmot Review; Healthy People 2020; County Health Rankings launch.
  16. 2016Chetty et al.: 14.6-year (men) and 10.1-year (women) life expectancy gap, richest vs poorest 1%.
  17. 2022CalAIM ECM & Community Supports; Medi-Cal CHW and Peer Support benefits.
  18. 2023–24Medicaid unwinding: 25.2M disenrolled nationally, 69% procedural.
  19. 2025H.R.1 Medicaid work requirements and six-month renewals; CHR&R new model.

Sources: Engels (1845); Virchow (1848/2006); Du Bois (1899, 1906/2003); Lalonde (1974); WHO (1978, 1986, 2008); DHSS (1980); Marmot et al. (1984, 2010); Dahlgren & Whitehead (1991); Link & Phelan (1995); Pub. L. 109-18; Chetty et al. (2016); DHCS; KFF; CRS (2025).
