# Paper Walls

**Social Determinants of Health, Document Burden, Navigation Burden & Resource Access — a California / Medi-Cal lens**

Matthew A. Cochran · November 2026

An animated, data-centered presentation explaining the problem ZipShift Navigator is designed to address: the help exists, but paperwork and navigation stand between people and the services they qualify for.

- **Presentation:** open `index.html` (keyboard arrows, swipe, or scroll).
- **Downloads:** `downloads/Paper-Walls.pptx` (slide deck), `downloads/Paper-Walls-Bibliography.pdf` / `.docx` (APA 7 references, 143 entries).
- **Data:** chart series in `data-*.csv`, drawn from the cited public sources.

Figures were checked against primary sources where possible; caveats are shown as footnotes on each slide. Research and drafting were AI-assisted and reviewed by the author.
